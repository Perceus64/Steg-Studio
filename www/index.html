For learning. LSB steganography is easy to detect (that's what the Analyze tab shows) and is destroyed the moment an image is re-compressed as JPEG or resized — so always keep and share the PNG. The AES-256-GCM layer means that even if someone suspects data is present, they can't read it without the password. These trade-offs are the point of the demo.
